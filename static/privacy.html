PropVibe Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

What PropVibe does

PropVibe is a tool used by real-estate agents to generate AI property listing posts — images and captions — from a set of listing details and photos, and publish them to a connected Facebook Page.

Information we collect

  • Property listing details provided by the agent using the tool (photos, price, location, bedrooms/bathrooms).
  • Aggregate click-tracking data on shared listing links (how many times a link was clicked).
  • Engagement data (likes and comments) read back from the connected Facebook Page for published posts.

Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect any personal data from people who click a shared listing link. We only record an aggregate click count — we do not identify, profile, or track individual visitors.

How data is stored

Listing posts and engagement records are stored in Airtable, which PropVibe uses as its backend data store.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@propvibe.example.

PropVibe is a student hackathon project. This policy is provided to describe the app's data practices in plain terms and is not intended as a legal document.
