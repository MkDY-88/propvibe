Business Model

How PropVibe Works As a Business

The problem

Every property agent posts on social media. Almost none of them know whether what they posted actually worked — and even when they do, nothing about their process improves tomorrow because of it. Marketing stays static while the market moves.

Who it's for

Individual agents and small agencies — not large enterprise firms. This is the “AI Solopreneur” model applied directly: one person, running the marketing output of a full team, through one system.

How it makes money

A simple monthly subscription, priced by posting volume — positioned well below the cost of even a part-time social media assistant or freelance designer. Heavier users (more listings, more posts) pay more; light users pay less. No large upfront cost, no long-term contract required.

Why it scales

Every AI-driven step in this system — caption generation, poster creation, lighting/weather image variants — is built cache-first: each unique combination is generated once, ever, and reused after that. This isn't just a performance choice, it's a deliberate cost-control decision, so unit economics stay predictable per listing rather than growing unbounded with usage. The backend itself is stateless per request, so serving more agents doesn't require re-architecting anything.

On scope

Some of what we built goes beyond what a working agent strictly needs day to day — we built it anyway, because it was part of the brief, and because knowing what's essential versus what's a nice-to-have is itself part of good product judgment. Everything extra is still built to the same cost-bounded, cache-first standard as the core system.
